About

I'm Subigya Ghimire, a security researcher based in Nepal. My work sits across penetration testing, red teaming, OSINT, and threat detection — the practical edges of cybersecurity, where curiosity meets discipline.

The goal isn't just to break things. It's to understand a system well enough to explain the failure clearly, and to help build something sturdier in its place. I write about that work here and on Medium, aimed at a general audience rather than only other practitioners.

This site is a deliberately quiet record of that work — details are shared on my own terms, in keeping with the same privacy principles I write about.

Focus areas

  • Penetration testing and vulnerability assessment
  • OSINT and exposure mapping
  • Red teaming and detection engineering
  • Digital privacy and everyday security education
